## q_commerce_definition

Quick commerce (q-commerce) is an ultrafast delivery model that promises delivery of everyday goods — groceries, snacks, medicines, etc. — typically within 10–30 minutes. It relies on a network of small, strategically located warehouses called "dark stores" (not open to the public) placed within densely populated urban areas. Source: https://example.com/q-commerce-india-2024, https://example.com/q-commerce-geography
